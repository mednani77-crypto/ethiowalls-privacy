Request deletion of EthioWalls data

EthioWalls does not require an account. This page explains how to remove local data and request removal of a content report you submitted.

Data on your device

You can remove favorites and clear recent searches in the app. To remove all app-private settings, viewed wallpaper IDs, queued reports, catalog data, and cached images, clear EthioWalls app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Android backup may restore app data under your device settings. Wallpapers saved to your gallery must be deleted from the gallery separately.

Reports sent to EthioWalls

To request deletion of a report you submitted, email hafedmohamad28@gmail.com with the wallpaper name or ID and approximate date of the report. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information. Reports have no account ID, so these details help us locate the right record. We will respond to your request and manually remove a matching report from the moderation database. Until removal, submitted report records are retained for content moderation; the report service does not store your IP address in that database.

Read the EthioWalls Privacy Policy for more details.
