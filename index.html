EthioWalls Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

EthioWalls is an independent wallpaper app. You can browse and use it without creating an account. This policy describes the current Android app and its supporting content services. For privacy questions or requests, email hafedmohamad28@gmail.com.

Information kept on your device

The app stores your language and display settings, favorites, followed collections, recent searches, viewed and previously shown wallpaper IDs, download history, automatic wallpaper settings and history, and a cached catalog and images. These records support offline use and personalize which wallpapers appear. They are stored in the app's local storage and are not sent to EthioWalls as a browsing history. Gallery downloads remain in your device's photo storage until you remove them. If Android backup is enabled, your device or backup provider may back up app data under its own settings.

Network requests and service providers

To show current wallpapers, the app requests public catalog data from Supabase and image files from their listed hosts, primarily Cloudflare R2; some legacy images may use other image hosts. These services necessarily receive standard connection information such as your IP address and request details and may keep operational logs under their own policies. EthioWalls does not attach an account ID or your locally saved favorites, searches, or viewed history to catalog or image requests.

Optional content reports

If you report a wallpaper, the app sends its ID, your selected reason, an optional note that you write, and the app version to an EthioWalls report endpoint hosted by Supabase. Reports may remain queued on your device until delivery succeeds. The report service uses the request IP address temporarily to limit abuse; the reports database stores the report fields, not that IP address. Please do not include personal information in a report note. Submitted reports remain in the moderation database until manually removed; you can request removal using the contact address below.

Other app actions

Setting a wallpaper uses Android's wallpaper service. Saving a wallpaper writes an image to your gallery. Sharing opens Android's share sheet and gives the image and its title to the app you choose. Optional wallpaper notifications and automatic wallpaper changes are handled on your device. The current app contains no advertising SDK, account sign-in, or active usage analytics service.

Your choices and deletion

You can clear recent searches in the app, change notification and automatic wallpaper settings, or remove favorites. Clearing the app's storage removes its local preferences, cached images, and queued reports. Uninstalling normally removes app-private data, although Android backup may restore it; gallery downloads must be removed separately. To ask about or request deletion of a submitted report, email hafedmohamad28@gmail.com with the wallpaper and approximate report date. Because reports are submitted without an account, we may need those details to locate one.

Security and changes

The app uses HTTPS for its catalog, image, and report requests and Android app-private storage for local records. We may update this policy when the app's data practices change; the effective date above will change accordingly. We will update the policy before adding advertising or analytics data collection.
